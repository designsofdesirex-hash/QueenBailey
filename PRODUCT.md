# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: plain static HTML/CSS/JS for a zero-backend, low-friction personal site.

## Users

Existing and prospective followers of Bailey's public X persona who want to follow her, understand the persona, or send direct financial support / gifts.

## Product Purpose

Turn Bailey's public X presence and payment ecosystem into a focused, premium destination that makes it easy to follow the live persona and choose a direct tribute path.

## Positioning

The site is a digital extension of the existing Bailey persona, not a conventional service marketplace: Bailey is the personality, Bailey's Bank is the money-facing ritual, and X remains the live channel.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from social media or direct sharing, scan a short editorial narrative, and leave to external destinations for payment, gifting, wishlist shopping, or following on X.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Fully static; no backend, accounts, forms, checkout, or custom-service booking flow. External destinations include X accounts, Throne, Cash App, Venmo, LoyalFans, Amazon wishlist, and Bitcoin. The page must not invent services, pricing, social proof, or private content.

## Brand Commitments

Public persona language supplied by the user includes “6'1 Queen,” “Your new found religion,” “You only exist to make me richer,” “Wealth Magnet,” Bailey's Bank, Alpha, cucks, subs, and femsubs. The tone is dominant, status-focused, bratty, sexually suggestive, and adult-only.

## Evidence on Hand

Public X profiles: @BaileyisBratty and @baileysbank. Payment / support destinations supplied by the user. No approved local photography or DM screenshots are present in the project; the build must use authored abstract editorial imagery or clearly marked replacement slots rather than fabricate photographs or screenshots.

## Product Principles

- Make the website feel like Bailey's world instead of explaining a business model she does not publicly present.
- Put the tribute action within seconds of arrival.
- Keep X as the live, changing source of personality and proof.
- Treat support links as deliberate ritual actions, not a generic link list.
- Keep the surface compact enough that its value feels concentrated.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect adult-only framing, maintain readable contrast, provide visible keyboard focus, preserve semantic link labels, and support reduced motion.
